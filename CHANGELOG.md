## 0.49.0 (2026-10-12)

- **3D shapes**, among the materials (Infantil to Bachillerato): prisms (cube, cuboid, triangular, pentagonal and
  hexagonal), pyramids (triangular, square, pentagonal and hexagonal), round solids (cylinder, cone and sphere) and the
  regular polyhedra, drawn by the board itself, without a 3D library. They turn with a finger, the mouse or the arrow
  keys, come closer with the wheel or two fingers, and can turn by themselves.
- «Unfold» opens a shape slowly into its net and shows it from above; «Fold» closes it, and a slider stops it halfway.
  The nets are the classic ones: the cross of the cube, the strip of the prisms with a base on each side, the star of
  the pyramids, the sector of the cone, the two flowers of the dodecahedron. «Another net» goes through all the nets
  of the small shapes (the 11 of the cube and of the octahedron, the 2 of the tetrahedron) and makes new ones for the
  big ones. The sphere cannot be unfolded, and says so.
- «Count»: the board numbers the faces, the edges or the vertices one by one (another tap, all at once), seen through.
  The name of the shape with its faces, edges and vertices (or what a round solid has), and in Secondary
  «Faces + vertices − edges», which is always 2.
- A tap paints a face, and its colour stays in the net; «See through» shows the hidden edges dashed; the views from the
  front, from above and from a side are drawn without perspective.
- Each school level starts with its own shape, and Infantil only offers the six it works with. It is shown on sites
  that had already chosen their materials, with its teacher's guide.

## 0.48.0 (2026-10-12)

- **Clap echo**, a new game (Infantil to Secondary): the board counts four and claps a rhythm of one or two bars
  (ta and ti-ti, or harder, with ta-a, a rest and ti-ri-ti-ri), slow or at normal speed, and the class claps it back.
  The echo can be tapped on a big drum on the board (or with the space bar): the board compares the taps with the
  rhythm, allowing for a slightly faster or slower echo; or the teacher, listening, says «Well done!». Then the rhythm
  shows written with its syllables, each beat green or red. A streak of echoes and the record; «Again» and «Another
  rhythm». It is shown on sites that had already chosen their games, with its teacher's guide.

## 0.45.0 (2026-10-12)

- **Duel in the materials** (the rods, the number line, the fraction wall, the geoboard, the base 10 blocks and the
  balance scale): «Duel» puts the same material in two halves, for two students with the same challenge, written on
  top. «At once»: both at the same time (on a multi-touch screen each half takes its own fingers) and the first to
  press «Done!» wins. «In turns»: one after the other, each with their own clock (for a projector or a screen of one

  neither in the browser nor in the course, and does not take the phone's orders or count stickers.

## 0.39.0 (2026-10-12)

- **The calculator, redesigned for class.** «Step by step» works out a combined operation line by line, one level of
  the order of operations at a time (brackets, then powers, roots and functions, then × and ÷, then + and −; the ones
  of the same level together), with what is about to be done underlined and what has just been worked out in green;
  on by default in Primary. «Estimate first» hides the result («?») until «=» is pressed again, so the class estimates
  before checking. The basic calculator has brackets, the operations in solid blue and a wide «=»; the scientific one
  has a «2nd» key for the inverse functions (sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹, x³, ∛, eˣ, 10ˣ) and «×10ˣ» for powers of ten, and
  shows DEG or RAD on the display. Negative numbers read as numbers (−2² = −4).
- **Simon with the syllables of a word** (Spanish and English for now): the pads show the syllables of a word and some
  others, and the word is the sequence: its syllables light up while it builds up on the board, or it is written whole
  for the class to split it. The words grow from two syllables to five; backwards, the word is said from the end; in
  the Infantil mode, in capitals. The content packs can bring their words split into syllables (`syllables`).
- The teacher's guides of the calculator and of Simon, updated (Martínez and Goikoetxea, 2020, on syllables in
  Spanish).

## 0.38.0 (2026-10-12)

- **Exit ticket**, in «Live»: at the end of the lesson each student says from their device how they are getting on
  (four faces, from «not yet» to «could explain it to someone», adapted from Marzano's 0–3 scale) and answers one to
  three short questions, written by the teacher or chosen from ideas grouped by what they ask (what they understood,
  how they worked, how the lesson went, for the teacher). While it is open the board shows only who has handed it in,
  and the device turns green to show at the door. Once closed: the faces of the class in bars, and the answers, which
  do not show until the teacher asks for them, in piles by face («not yet» first), with names only on request, one at
  random without a name to talk about it, cards that can be taken off the screen, and a download as a spreadsheet.
  It can be reopened, and another ticket starts from the last one.
- The teacher's guide of the exit ticket: what it is for, why (formative assessment: Black and Wiliam, 1998; the four
  kinds of prompts of Marzano, 2012; the exit pass of Wiliam, 2011), how to take it to class, with and without
  devices, and what it gives at each stage.
- The answers of live sessions have room for sentences (255 characters).
